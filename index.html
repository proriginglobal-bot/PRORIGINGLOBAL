Trade Knowledge Base

B2B Export FAQ & Trade Compliance

Instant answers regarding minimum order quantities, phytosanitary certifications, Incoterms, OEM private labeling, and global ocean freight documentation.

🔍

What is the Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) for ocean freight export?

+

Our standard commercial MOQ is 1 x 20ft General Purpose container (~14 Metric Tonnes) or 1 x 40ft High Cube container (~26 Metric Tonnes). For first-time trial evaluations or distributor testing, mixed product stuffing (e.g., 5kg blocks combined with 650g briquettes and husk chips) is fully supported within a single 40ft HC container.

What is the technical difference between Unwashed, Washed, and Super-Buffered Cocopeat?

+

Unwashed (High EC): Raw coir pith with naturally occurring sodium and potassium salts (EC > 1.5 mS/cm), suitable for industrial absorption or outdoor landscape mulching.

Washed (Low EC): Thoroughly desalinated with deep-bore freshwater flumes to achieve EC < 0.8 mS/cm.

Super-Buffered (Calcium Nitrate Treated): Undergoes chemical cation exchange displacement where Calcium and Magnesium displace bonded Sodium (Na) and Potassium (K) ions, locking EC below < 0.45 mS/cm and pH between 5.8 – 6.5. This prevents nutrient lockout in sensitive greenhouse hydroponics.

Can you manufacture custom OEM private label packaging with our brand artwork?

+

Yes. Over 65% of our global export volume is custom-manufactured under private label agreements for international fertilizer brands, hydroponic retailers, and greenhouse chains. We provide rotogravure printed polybags, full-color shrink wraps, custom barcode / UPC labels, outer master cartons, and branded corner protectors on export pallets.

What international export documentation and phytosanitary certificates accompany each shipment?

+

Every export container leaves port with a complete legalized documentation dossier including: Official Government Phytosanitary Certificate (Zero Pests/Pathogens), ISPM-15 Heat-Treatment / Fumigation Certificate (Methyl Bromide or Heat), Certificate of Origin (Form A / COO), Commercial Invoice & Detailed Packing List, Clean On-Board Ocean Bill of Lading (B/L), and our In-House Laboratory Batch Certificate of Analysis (COA).

What payment terms and trade finance options do you offer for commercial buyers?

+

We offer transparent international commercial payment terms: 1. Irrevocable Letter of Credit (L/C at sight) confirmed by prime international banks for volume contracts. 2. Telegraphic Transfer (T/T) (30% advance deposit upon PO confirmation, 70% against scanned shipping documents and Bill of Lading). For long-standing contracted corporate accounts, structured credit lines and escrow arrangements are available.

Can we mix different coconut products into a single 40ft High Cube container?

+

Absolutely. Mixed container stuffing is one of our key advantages for international importers and distributors. You can combine 5kg Cocopeat Blocks, 650g Briquettes, Husk Chips bales, and Activated Carbon sacks within the same 40ft HC container to optimize your inventory turnover and minimize shipping costs.

How do you guarantee moisture levels stay below 15% to prevent mold during ocean shipping?

+

Our substrates undergo raised concrete solar drying and continuous rotary moisture calibration until internal pith moisture reaches 12% – 14%. Every container is lined with heavy-gauge polyethylene film and equipped with industrial Calcium Chloride desiccant moisture trap bags hanging inside the container ribs to eliminate condensation risks during 30+ day ocean voyages.

What are your production lead times from contract confirmation to container loading?

+

Standard unbranded 5kg blocks and 650g bricks are typically stuffed and dispatched to Cochin / Tuticorin port within 7 to 10 business days. Custom private label printed packaging orders take approximately 14 to 18 business days for initial cylinder engraving and production.

Section 02 • Manufacturing & Thermal Activation

Two-Stage Physical Steam Activation

PR ORIGIN GLOBAL

PR Origin Global employs exclusively physical thermal activation (superheated steam and controlled flue gas) rather than chemical etching agents, eliminating residual zinc chloride or phosphoric acid contaminants.

1 Carbonization Stage
Raw mature coconut shells undergo slow pyrolysis in oxygen-limited rotary kilns (500°C–650°C), driving off volatile organic matters and moisture to yield high-purity, fixed-carbon base char.
2 Steam Activation (900°C–1100°C)
Base charcoal is introduced into computerized vertical fluidized kilns under high-pressure steam at 900°C–1100°C. Controlled oxidation etches a vast network of molecular pores throughout the matrix.
